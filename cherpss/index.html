    A LITTLE CHESS. A LITTLE CHAOS.

    How to play

    01 · Goal

    Take the enemy king, or leave them without champions.

    02 · The board

    Chess moves, including castling, en passant and promotion. There is no check: kings can be challenged.

    03 · Captures are duels

    Both players secretly pick Rock, Scissors or Paper, then fight. Win, and the capture happens; lose, and your piece is removed instead. Each side has four champions of each kind, and a champion that loses is gone.

    04 · Controls

    White: WASD move · F attack · G guard · H special
    Black: arrows move · J attack · K guard · L special
    Controllers: stick · A attack · X guard · B special
    Phones: tap to pick, battles play themselves.

    05 · Helpers

    Pieces around the contested square help their side in the duel: Pawn shield · Knight speed · Bishop quicker, stronger special · Rook stronger special · Queen faster attacks · King more damage.
